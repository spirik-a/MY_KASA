Questions, answered

Can CyberShield see my passwords?

No. Your vault is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your master password. We only ever store encrypted data.

What if I forget my master password?

You can set up a recovery key during sign-up. Teams admins can also enable account recovery for employees.

Can I import my existing passwords?

Yes. Import from browsers and most password managers using a CSV file. It takes about a minute.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account page, and export your data whenever you like.
